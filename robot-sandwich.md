# **How to Make a Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich**

## Assumptions
- The robot is fully charged and has a humanoid construction.
- We already have a full countertop with the following items on it:
    -  Butter Knife
    -  Open Jar of Peanut Butter
    -  Open Jar of Grape Jelly
    -  2 slices of bread lying in a plate next to each other


## Instructions

1. Position self in front of items 

2. Use right hand to pick up the butter knife by the handle end

3. Pick up open jar of peanut butter using left hand

4. Turn the butter knife so that the point is tipped down at the open jar of peanut butter

5. Stick butter knife into the jar of peanut butter and make a slight twist and push motion to get globdules of peanut butter to stick to the knife

6. Pull the butter knife out of the peanut butter jar ensuring that you have peanut butter on the knife

7. Set down the peanut butter jar in your left hand and use your left hand to pick up the leftmost slice of bread.

8. Firmly press the knife to the slice of bread in your left hand, and reapeatedly slide the knife over the top of the bread until all of the peanut butter coats the bread's largest area.

9. Set down the slice of bread coated in Peanut butter back in its previous position, with the coated side facing upward.

10. Pick up open jar of grape jelly using left hand

11. Turn the butter knife so that the point is tipped down at the open jar of grape jelly

12. Stick butter knife into the jar of Grape Jelly and make a slight twist and push motion to get globdules of Grape Jelly to stick to the knife

13. Pull the butter knife out of the Grape Jelly jar ensuring that you have Grape Jelly on the knife

14. Set down the Grape Jelly jar in your left hand and use your left hand to pick up the rightmost slice of bread.

15. Firmly press the knife to the slice of bread in your left hand, and reapeatedly slide the knife over the top of the bread until all of the Grape Jelly coats the bread's largest area.

16. Take the slice of bread with jelly coated on its surface and place it upon the slice of bread with Peanut butter with the jelly and the peanut butter facing each other.

17. Take the knife in your right hand and cut the sandwich corner to corner.

18. Replace all ingredients in the places where they are stores long term after closing them.

## Done When
- Sandwich is constructed and cut in half
- Area is cleared of all ingredients and utensils besides plate and sandwich
